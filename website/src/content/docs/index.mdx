    <div class="feature-card" style="--card-accent: #6ba89a; --icon-bg: rgba(107,168,154,0.1); --icon-color: #6ba89a; --icon-bg-dark: rgba(107,168,154,0.18); --icon-color-dark: #94c4b8;">
      <div class="feature-icon">⬡</div>
      <h3 class="feature-title">Server-Independent</h3>
      <p class="feature-description">
        Every component works if all centralized servers go offline. P2P via Kademlia DHT,
        QUIC transport, gossipsub mesh. Zero single points of failure.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature-card" style="--card-accent: #c47a6c; --icon-bg: rgba(196,122,108,0.1); --icon-color: #c47a6c; --icon-bg-dark: rgba(196,122,108,0.18); --icon-color-dark: #d9a295;">
      <div class="feature-icon">△</div>
      <h3 class="feature-title">AI-Native Runtime</h3>
      <p class="feature-description">
        On-chain AI verification with off-chain inference. Model registry, compute market,
        and attestation protocol built into L1 — not bolted on.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature-card" style="--card-accent: #b8975a; --icon-bg: rgba(184,151,90,0.1); --icon-color: #b8975a; --icon-bg-dark: rgba(184,151,90,0.18); --icon-color-dark: #d4bc8a;">
      <div class="feature-icon">⊞</div>
      <h3 class="feature-title">Dual VM Execution</h3>
      <p class="feature-description">
        WASM and EVM side by side. Deploy in Rust, AssemblyScript, or Solidity.
        One chain, two ecosystems. No bridges needed.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature-card" style="--card-accent: #2e3a5c; --icon-bg: rgba(46,58,92,0.1); --icon-color: #2e3a5c; --icon-bg-dark: rgba(46,58,92,0.2); --icon-color-dark: #7888b0;">
      <div class="feature-icon">⊘</div>

      <p class="feature-description">

        Cross-compiles to any target. redb + BLAKE3.
      </p>
    </div>
    <div class="feature-card" style="--card-accent: #6b4c9a; --icon-bg: rgba(107,76,154,0.1); --icon-color: #6b4c9a; --icon-bg-dark: rgba(139,108,184,0.18); --icon-color-dark: #9b7cc8;">

        stateless validation and instant light client sync.
      </p>
