THE PROBLEM

Four barriers holding back decentralized AI

Centralized AI Compute

AWS, Google, and Microsoft control 80%+ of AI infrastructure. A single point of failure for the entire AI economy.

Wasted Mining Energy

Proof of Work blockchains burn electricity solving meaningless puzzles. Proof of Stake requires nothing. Neither creates real-world value.

No Decentralized Inference

AI developers cannot access affordable, censorship-resistant inference at scale. Every model call routes through centralized APIs.

AI as Afterthought

No existing blockchain treats AI inference as a first-class protocol operation. It is always a bolt-on, never native.
