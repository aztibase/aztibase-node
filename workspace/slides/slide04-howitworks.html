→

2

POST TASK

Requester posts inference job, pays AZTB reward

→

3

INFERENCE

Assigned validator runs inference via tract runtime

→

4

ATTESTATION

Quorum of 2+ validators verify matching results

→

5

SETTLEMENT

Rewards split among validators, 5% royalty to model provider

Validator Scoring: PoUW Formula

Validators are ranked by a multi-metric score: Accuracy (40%) + Latency (30%) + Availability (30%). Higher-scoring validators receive more task assignments and earn proportionally more rewards. This creates a competitive market for inference quality.
