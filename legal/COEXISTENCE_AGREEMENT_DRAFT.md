# TRADEMARK COEXISTENCE AGREEMENT

**DRAFT — FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW BEFORE USE**

---

This Trademark Coexistence Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of __________ ("Effective Date") by and between:

**Party A:**
Aztibase (Pty) Ltd, a private company incorporated in South Africa under the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC) in 2012, operating the Aztibase Network blockchain protocol ("Aztibase Network");

**Party B:**
Aztibase Systems Inc., a company incorporated in the United Kingdom in 2023, with its principal place of business at New Malden, United Kingdom ("Aztibase Systems");

collectively referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party."

---

## RECITALS

A. Aztibase Network has been incorporated and operating under the "Aztibase" name since 2012, predating Aztibase Systems' incorporation by approximately eleven (11) years.

B. Aztibase Network uses the mark "Aztibase" and related marks, including the ticker symbol "AZTB," in connection with a Layer-1 blockchain protocol, cryptocurrency, decentralized consensus software, and related financial technology services (collectively, "Blockchain Products"). Aztibase Network holds the primary domain aztibase.com.

C. Aztibase Systems uses the mark "Aztibase" and related marks in connection with software development tools, software development kits (SDKs), AI web agent technology, and browser extensions (collectively, "AI Agent Products").

D. The Parties recognize that their respective products and services operate in distinct markets, serve different consumer bases, and are not likely to cause consumer confusion.

E. The Parties desire to formalize their coexistence and set forth the terms under which they may each continue to use the "Aztibase" mark in their respective fields without conflict, recognizing Aztibase Network's prior use and seniority in the mark.

F. This Agreement is entered into on a mutual, no-fee basis. Neither Party shall owe any payment, royalty, licensing fee, or other monetary consideration to the other Party under this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Aztibase Network Marks"** means the trademarks, service marks, trade names, and associated logos used by Aztibase Network in connection with Blockchain Products, including but not limited to "Aztibase Network," "Aztibase," the ticker symbol "AZTB," and variations thereof.

1.2 **"Aztibase Systems Marks"** means the trademarks, service marks, trade names, and associated logos used by Aztibase Systems in connection with AI Agent Products, including but not limited to "Aztibase Systems" and variations thereof as registered or used in commerce.

1.3 **"Field of Use"** means the specific product category and market segment in which each Party is authorized to use the Aztibase marks, as defined in Section 2.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF USE

### 2.1 Aztibase Network — Permitted Field of Use

Aztibase Network shall have the exclusive right to use the Aztibase marks in connection with:

(a) Layer-1 blockchain protocol software for decentralized consensus;
(b) Cryptocurrency and digital asset services using the ticker symbol "AZTB";
(c) Distributed ledger technology, node software, and validator infrastructure;
(d) Decentralized application (dApp) platforms and smart contract execution;
(e) Cryptocurrency wallets, staking services, and related financial technology;
(f) Decentralized AI inference verification and compute markets operating on the blockchain;
(g) Any natural extension of the foregoing within the blockchain, cryptocurrency, and decentralized finance market.

### 2.2 Aztibase Systems — Permitted Field of Use

Aztibase Systems shall have the right to use the Aztibase Systems Marks in connection with:

(a) Software development tools and SDKs for building AI-powered web agents;
(b) Browser extensions for web automation and AI agent deployment;
(c) Cloud-computing software for AI agent orchestration;
(d) Related developer tools and documentation;
(e) Any natural extension of the foregoing within the artificial intelligence and software development tools market.

### 2.3 Non-Encroachment

(a) Aztibase Systems agrees not to use the Aztibase Systems Marks in connection with blockchain protocols, cryptocurrency, digital asset issuance, distributed ledger technology, decentralized finance, token-based financial services, or any product or service that could reasonably be associated with the blockchain and cryptocurrency industry.

(b) Aztibase Network agrees not to use the Aztibase Network Marks in connection with standalone AI web agent SDKs, standalone browser-based automation tools, or standalone AI agent orchestration platforms that do not operate on or in connection with blockchain technology.

(c) For the avoidance of doubt, Aztibase Network's use of AI inference as a feature within its blockchain protocol (including on-chain AI verification, decentralized compute markets, AI model registries, and browser wallet extensions for blockchain transactions) shall not constitute encroachment into Aztibase Systems' Field of Use.

---

## 3. PRIOR USE ACKNOWLEDGMENT

3.1 Aztibase Systems acknowledges that Aztibase (Pty) Ltd was incorporated in 2012 and has used the "Aztibase" name in commerce since that date, establishing prior use rights in the mark.

3.2 Aztibase Systems acknowledges that its own incorporation in 2023 postdates Aztibase Network's prior use by approximately eleven (11) years.

3.3 Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as a waiver of Aztibase Network's prior use rights or seniority in the "Aztibase" mark. In the event of any ambiguity regarding the scope of permitted use under this Agreement, such ambiguity shall be resolved in favor of Aztibase Network as the senior user of the mark.

---

## 4. TRADEMARK REGISTRATIONS

4.1 Each Party acknowledges and consents to the other Party's existing and future trademark registrations within their respective Fields of Use, including:

(a) Aztibase Network's current and future applications in International Classes 9 (blockchain protocol software), 36 (cryptocurrency financial services), and 42 (blockchain SaaS platform services);

(b) Aztibase Systems' existing registrations in International Class 9 (downloadable computer software, web browser extensions, cloud-computing software) as they relate exclusively to AI Agent Products.

4.2 Neither Party shall oppose, petition to cancel, or otherwise challenge the other Party's trademark applications or registrations that fall within the other Party's Field of Use as defined in Section 2.

4.3 If a trademark office issues an office action or examination objection citing one Party's marks against the other Party's application, both Parties agree to cooperate in good faith, including by providing letters of consent or other supporting documentation to the relevant trademark office, to facilitate registration within the respective Fields of Use.

4.4 Each Party agrees to use commercially reasonable efforts to include distinguishing elements (such as "Systems" or "Network") in their respective branding to minimize any potential consumer confusion.

---

## 5. NO FINANCIAL CONSIDERATION

5.1 This Agreement is entered into on a mutual, reciprocal basis. No payment, royalty, licensing fee, or other monetary consideration is owed by either Party to the other under this Agreement.

5.2 Each Party bears its own costs and expenses in connection with the negotiation, execution, and performance of this Agreement, including any legal fees.

---

## 6. QUALITY AND REPUTATION

6.1 Each Party shall maintain the quality of goods and services offered under their respective marks at a level consistent with professional standards in their industry.

6.2 Neither Party shall use the Aztibase mark in a manner that is likely to tarnish, dilute, or bring into disrepute the other Party's mark or reputation.

6.3 If either Party becomes aware of third-party infringement of the "Aztibase" mark, it shall promptly notify the other Party.

---

## 7. GEOGRAPHIC SCOPE

7.1 This Agreement applies worldwide. Neither Party's use of the Aztibase mark shall be geographically restricted, provided that each Party operates within its respective Field of Use.

7.2 Each Party acknowledges the other Party's right to register and use its marks in any jurisdiction, subject to the Field of Use restrictions in Section 2.

---

## 8. DOMAIN NAMES AND ONLINE PRESENCE

8.1 Each Party acknowledges the other Party's right to maintain domain names and online presences incorporating "Aztibase," provided such use is within the respective Party's Field of Use.

8.2 Aztibase Systems acknowledges that Aztibase Network holds the domain aztibase.com and may acquire additional domains incorporating "Aztibase" for blockchain and cryptocurrency purposes.

8.3 Aztibase Network acknowledges that Aztibase Systems may hold domain names including but not limited to aztibase.ai and related domains for AI agent tool purposes.

---

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue in perpetuity unless terminated in accordance with this Section.

9.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon twelve (12) months' written notice to the other Party, provided that:

(a) Termination shall not affect trademark registrations obtained during the term of this Agreement;

(b) Both Parties shall retain the right to continue using marks registered or established during the term of this Agreement within their respective Fields of Use;

(c) Upon termination, the prior use rights of Aztibase Network as established in Section 3 shall remain fully intact and unaffected.

9.3 This Agreement shall automatically terminate with respect to a Party if that Party:

(a) Ceases to exist as a legal entity;
(b) Assigns all of its trademark rights to a third party, unless the assignee elects to assume this Agreement in writing within thirty (30) days of the assignment.

---

(a) It has full power and authority to enter into this Agreement;
(b) The execution and performance of this Agreement does not conflict with any other agreement to which it is a party;
(c) It is the owner or authorized user of the marks it purports to use under this Agreement.

10.2 Aztibase Network additionally represents that it was incorporated in South Africa in 2012 and has continuously used the "Aztibase" name in commerce since that date.

---

## 11. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

11.1 The Parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute arising under this Agreement through good faith negotiation for a period of thirty (30) days.

11.2 If the dispute is not resolved through negotiation, the Parties agree to submit the dispute to mediation administered by a mutually agreed-upon mediation service.

11.3 If mediation fails, the dispute shall be resolved by binding arbitration under the rules of the London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA), with the arbitration to take place in London, United Kingdom.

11.4 Each Party shall bear its own costs in any dispute, unless the arbitrator determines that one Party's position was frivolous or brought in bad faith, in which case the arbitrator may award reasonable attorneys' fees and costs to the prevailing Party.
