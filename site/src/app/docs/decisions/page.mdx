## Decision Process

All non-obvious technical choices go through this process:
1. Identify the decision needed
2. Research alternatives
3. Document trade-offs
4. Record the decision with rationale
5. Review during security audit

Full ADR details are available in the [source repository](https://github.com/aztibase/aztibase-node).
